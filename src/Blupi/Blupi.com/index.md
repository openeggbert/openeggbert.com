## Introduction

Blupi.com is the web domain owned by the

[Epsitec](Blupi/Epsitec/index.html)

company in the
        past.

<br>

*It looks, like Epsitec lost ownership of blupi.com in 2006.*

## Blupi.com in 1999

Domain blupi.com was bought for the first time probably in 1998 by the Epsitec company.

<br>

First archive.org snapshot for blupi.com was created on 6th December 1998. (

[https://web.archive.org/web/19981206200509/blupi.com](https://web.archive.org/web/19981206200509/blupi.com)

)

<br>

It is not known, if the domain was bought earlier than in 1999.

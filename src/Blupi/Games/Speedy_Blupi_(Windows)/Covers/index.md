Both

[Speedy Blupi](Blupi/Games/Speedy_Blupi_(Windows)/index.html)

I/II and

[Speedy Eggbert](Blupi/Games/Speedy_Eggbert/index.html)

1/2 were sold in the form of shiny

[CD-ROM](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

boxes and may look good on the shelves.

<br>

All these boxes shows big

[Blupi](Blupi/index.html)

/

[Eggbert](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

on the front side.

<br>

Back side is full of screenshots and game features.

- Back side also includes a slogan: Create your own levels.

After you buy a CD-ROM box, you need a computer, which meets the requirements and has a CD-ROM drive.

<br>

<br>

Speedy Blupi I/I needs the CD-ROM even after the installation.

- But there is a workaround for this, see: [Speedy Blupi Troubleshooting](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

Speedy Eggbert 1/2 does not need the CD-ROM even after the installation.

Planet Blupi is an enchanting strategy and adventure game for PC, seamlessly blending action with intellectually stimulating challenges. Beneath its calm and gentle exterior lies a captivating experience full of surprises. Perfect for players aged 12 to 99, it’s an engaging diversion that keeps you on your toes.

## System Requirements

- PC with Windows Vista/XP/2000/ME/98/95
- Pentium 90 with 16 MB RAM
- CD-ROM

## Storyline

Blupi lives peacefully on his planet until a mysterious meteorite crashes in the desert. Later, he notices his crops being destroyed by enormous spiders. Though usually tough and healthy, Blupi has been sneezing and coughing a lot lately. What’s happening? <br> The meteorite turns out to be a spaceship with a menacing robot onboard, whose mission is to build factories that create various aliens. You must help Blupi use his planet's resources to fend off these invaders!

## The Game

With about 30 missions ranging from simple to truly perplexing, your objectives vary from growing food to finding strategic locations to eliminating aliens. Some tasks might require just one Blupi, while others may need dozens!

- Help Blupi explore
- Cut trees to produce wood
- Transport materials
- Build houses
- Grow tomatoes
- Pick flowers
- Survey land
- Mine iron ore
- Set traps
- Build bridges
- Heal friends

## Intuitive Interface

Planet Blupi's interface is designed to be unobtrusive, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the game. No need for long mouse movements across the screen or endless button selections. A small area on the left provides mission information, and when you need Blupi to take action, simply click the appropriate spot in the scenery. A menu of possible actions appears, and you can make your choice smoothly and quickly. Optional exercises are available to help you learn the game.

## Varied Environments

Blupi’s planet offers four distinct areas:

- Open prairie
- Dense forests
- Deserts
- Snowy landscapes

Each mission takes place in a vast 40,000-cell area (200x200 square units). The full version even allows you to design your own scenery, set goals, and create custom missions.

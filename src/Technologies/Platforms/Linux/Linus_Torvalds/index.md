Linus Benedict Torvalds (born December 28,

[1969](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

) is a Finnish-American software engineer, best known for creating and leading the development of the

[Linux](Technologies/Platforms/Linux/index.html)

[kernel](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

. He is also the inventor of

[Git](Article_does_not_yet_exist_or_link_is_broken/index.html)

, a distributed version control system.

Blupi is a yellow round little character created by Daniel Roux in 1974 in the comics in his school books.

Blupi evolved into a beloved figure in many video games developed by Daniel Roux.

The latest Blupi appearance is in the game Blupi Is Back released in 2024.

Blupi is the registered trademark by the Epsitec company ( https://branddb.wipo.int/en/quicksearch/brand/CH501993000002322?sort=score%20desc&start=0&rows=30&asStructure=%7B%22_id%22:%2253d3%22,%22boolean%22:%22AND%22,%22bricks%22:%5B%7B%22_id%22:%2253d4%22,%22key%22:%22brandName%22,%22value%22:%22blupi%22,%22strategy%22:%22Simple%22%7D%5D%7D&=1718551274937&fg=_void&i=6 )

Original name of Blupi was Toto.

Blupi was renamed to Eggbert by the publisher company eGames, which is now a defunct shovelware publisher. eGames did not like the name Blupi.

The website blupi.com was the home for Blupi on the Internet, before Epsitec, its former owner, lost its ownership in 2006.

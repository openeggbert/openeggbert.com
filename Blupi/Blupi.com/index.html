Blupi.com

Introduction

Blupi.com is the web domain owned by the Epsitec company in the past.
It looks, like Epsitec lost ownership of blupi.com in 2006.

Blupi.com in 1999

Domain blupi.com was bought for the first time probably in 1998 by the Epsitec company.
First archive.org snapshot for blupi.com was created on 6th December 1998. (https://web.archive.org/web/19981206200509/blupi.com)
It is not known, if the domain was bought earlier than in 1999.

Blupi.com in 2006

Epsitec lost ownership of blupi.com in 2006. (https://web.archive.org/web/20060903123831/http://www.blupi.com/)
A new web content appeared: 
WELCOME TO

Blupi.com in 2014

In 2014 blupi.com had a web content probably for the last time (status as of the year 2024). (https://web.archive.org/web/20141028012058/http://www.blupi.com/)

Blupi.com in 2024 (status as of the day: 12th July 2024)

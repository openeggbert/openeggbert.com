Blupi is Back

Blupi is Back
Author: Daniel Roux
Release date: 23rd July 2024
Platforms: Android, Windows, Web
Blupi is Back is a Blupi game released in 2024 by its original author Daniel Roux, fifty years after Blupi's first appearance. Year 2024 is the Blupi's 50th anniversary.
Blupi is Back is a new Blupi game to be released in 20 year.

Supported platforms

Note: iOS (iPhone operating system) is not supported. Daniel stated, that iOS is too expensive to develop for.

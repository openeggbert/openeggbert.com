Fan projects

These are several Blupi fan projects.
These are applications developed by third party persons - not original developers of Speedy Blupi/Eggbert.
Daniel Roux, the original author of Speedy Blupi I/II and other games, mentioned, he is happy about these projects.
Fan projects and modifications are absolutely allowed and they appreciate the fans' enthusiasm. as long as the fans aren't doing it for money, it's all cool

Video on Youtube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iea_6DIFCmY

List of Fan projects

  • BlupiEdit - Tool for editing Blupi-related content.
  • tiled-blupi - Game development tool for Blupi projects.
  • X-Edit - Editing software for Blupi games.
  • X-Edit-Mapper - Mapping tool for Blupi games.
  • Speedy Eggbert - Godot prototype - Prototype demonstration video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KD7QKwXwzXQ
  • Eggbert 3 - Upcoming game in the Eggbert series.
  • Open Eggbert (2024 - development started) - New open-source project in development.
